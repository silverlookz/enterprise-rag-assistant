# Northwind Financial — Credit Card Dispute Handling Procedure

> Fictional sample document created for demonstration purposes.

## Scope
This procedure applies to all disputed transactions on Northwind personal and business credit cards.

## Filing Window
Cardholders must report a disputed transaction within 120 days of the statement date on which it first appeared. Disputes filed after 120 days are reviewed case by case and are not guaranteed.

## Provisional Credit
For disputes under 500 dollars, the agent issues a provisional credit immediately. For disputes of 500 dollars or more, provisional credit is issued within 10 business days once the cardholder submits the signed dispute form. Provisional credit is reversed if the merchant provides valid proof of the transaction.

## Fraud versus Merchant Disputes
Fraud claims, where the cardholder did not authorize the transaction, require the card to be blocked and reissued before the claim is opened. Merchant disputes, such as goods not received or not as described, require the cardholder to first attempt resolution with the merchant and provide evidence of that attempt.

## Resolution Timelines
The target resolution time is 45 days for merchant disputes and 30 days for fraud claims. Cardholders receive a written decision letter at the end of the investigation.
