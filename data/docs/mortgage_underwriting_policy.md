# Northwind Financial — Residential Mortgage Underwriting Policy

> Fictional sample document created for demonstration purposes.

## Purpose
This policy defines the minimum standards for underwriting residential mortgages originated by Northwind Financial retail branches and the broker channel.

## Loan-to-Value Limits
The maximum loan-to-value (LTV) ratio for an uninsured conventional mortgage is 80 percent of the lower of the purchase price or appraised value. Insured high-ratio mortgages may reach 95 percent LTV for owner-occupied properties with a purchase price under 1.5 million dollars. Rental properties are capped at 80 percent LTV regardless of insurance.

## Debt Service Ratios
Applicants must have a gross debt service (GDS) ratio no higher than 39 percent and a total debt service (TDS) ratio no higher than 44 percent. Ratios are calculated at the qualifying rate, which is the greater of the contract rate plus 2 percent or the benchmark floor rate of 5.25 percent.

## Income Verification
Salaried applicants must provide a recent pay stub and an employment letter dated within 30 days. Self-employed applicants must provide two years of tax assessments and business financial statements. Bonus and commission income is averaged over two years before it can be used for qualification.

## Credit Requirements
The minimum credit score for an insured mortgage is 600 for at least one borrower. Files with a score under 680 require a second underwriter review. Any bankruptcy must be discharged for at least two years before application.

## Exceptions
Exceptions to this policy require written approval from a Senior Credit Officer. Exceptions must be documented in the loan origination system with the business rationale and compensating factors.
