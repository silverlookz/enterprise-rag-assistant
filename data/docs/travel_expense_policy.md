# Northwind Financial — Travel and Expense Policy

> Fictional sample document created for demonstration purposes.

## Approval
Domestic travel requires manager approval in the expense system before booking. International travel requires approval from a Vice President.

## Air Travel
Economy class is required for flights under six hours. Premium economy is permitted for flights of six hours or longer. Business class requires Executive Vice President approval.

## Meals and Per Diems
The daily meal allowance is 85 dollars for domestic travel and 110 dollars for international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at approved client entertainment events.

## Submitting Expenses
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the trip end date with itemized receipts for any expense over 25 dollars. Reports submitted after 60 days require Finance approval.
