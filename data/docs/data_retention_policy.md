# Northwind Financial — Enterprise Data Retention and Classification Policy

> Fictional sample document created for demonstration purposes.

## Data Classification
All data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Client personal information and payment card data are always Restricted. Analytical datasets derived from client data are Confidential unless they are fully anonymized.

## Retention Periods
Client account records are retained for seven years after the relationship ends. Call recordings are retained for two years. Application logs are retained for 13 months. Data in analytics sandboxes must be deleted or refreshed after 90 days.

## Use of Data in AI and Analytics
Restricted data may not be sent to external AI services. Models and dashboards that use Confidential data must be registered in the model inventory before production use. Prompts and responses for internal AI assistants are logged and retained for 13 months for audit purposes.

## Legal Hold
When a legal hold is issued, scheduled deletion is suspended for all affected records until the Legal department releases the hold in writing.
