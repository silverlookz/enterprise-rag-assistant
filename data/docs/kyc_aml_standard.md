# Northwind Financial — Know Your Customer and Anti-Money Laundering Standard

> Fictional sample document created for demonstration purposes.

## Customer Identification
Every new client must be identified before an account is opened. Acceptable methods are a valid government-issued photo ID verified in person, the dual-process method using two independent reliable sources, or a credit file method where the file has existed for at least three years.

## Enhanced Due Diligence
Enhanced due diligence is required for politically exposed persons, clients from high-risk jurisdictions, and clients whose expected annual transaction volume exceeds 1 million dollars. Enhanced due diligence includes source-of-funds documentation and senior management sign-off.

## Transaction Reporting
Cash transactions of 10,000 dollars or more, in a single transaction or multiple transactions within 24 hours by the same client, must be reported as a large cash transaction within 15 calendar days. Suspicious transactions must be reported as soon as practicable once reasonable grounds for suspicion are established, regardless of the amount.

## Periodic Review
High-risk client profiles are reviewed annually. Medium-risk profiles are reviewed every three years, and low-risk profiles every five years.

## Record Keeping
Client identification records and transaction records must be retained for at least five years from the date the account is closed or the transaction is completed.
